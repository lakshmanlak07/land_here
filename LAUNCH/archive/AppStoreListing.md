## Description (3420 / 4,000 chars)

You got in. Now what?

LandHere turns your acceptance letter into a step-by-step action plan. No more Googling "what comes after my I-20" or saving 40 browser tabs you'll never read again. Answer a few questions about where you're from and where you're headed. LandHere builds a personalised visa and arrival roadmap for your specific corridor.

─────────────────────────────────────
THE PLAN — PERSONALISED FOR YOU
─────────────────────────────────────

Current live corridors:

→ India to USA (F-1 student visa)
→ India to Canada (study permit)
→ India to UK (student visa)

Each plan follows the exact steps in the right order, verified against official government sources — travel.state.gov, gov.uk, canada.ca — so you know what to do and when.

─────────────────────────────────────
WHAT'S INSIDE YOUR PLAN
─────────────────────────────────────

VISA ROADMAP
Every step from receiving your acceptance letter to clearing customs. Pay the MRV fee, book your consular appointment, prepare your documents, attend your interview, receive your passport, travel. Sequenced, explained, and sourced from the official government page for your corridor.

WORK RULES
How many hours you can legally work, on-campus vs. off-campus rules, and what documentation you need before you start. Verified against official immigration guidance for the US, UK, and Canada — not secondhand advice.

FIRST-WEEK CHECKLIST
Land and know exactly what to do: get your Social Security Number, SIN, or National Insurance Number; open a local bank account; register with your GP or health centre; activate your phone plan; check in with your university's international office. Built specifically around your destination.

SCHOLARSHIPS & FUNDING
A curated directory of scholarships available to international students, with direct links to apply. No paywalls, no sign-ups.

COUNTRY HIGHLIGHTS & FUN FACTS
Get to know your new home before you arrive — culture, cost of living, campus life, and verified facts about each destination country.

─────────────────────────────────────
YOUR JOURNEY, TRACKED
─────────────────────────────────────

Every step you complete is saved. Come back a week later and pick up exactly where you left off. The four phases — After Acceptance, Visa & Documents, Before Departure, After Arrival — keep you oriented so nothing slips through the cracks.

─────────────────────────────────────
WHY STUDENTS TRUST LANDHERE
─────────────────────────────────────

Every visa step is verified against the official source — not a blog, not a forum post, not secondhand advice. We cite travel.state.gov for the US, gov.uk for the UK, and canada.ca for Canada. If something has not been human-verified against an official source, we say so. We don't publish unverified immigration guidance.

LandHere does not show ads. It does not sell your data. It collects only what it needs to personalise your plan: your origin country, destination country, and university name.

COMING SOON
More corridors — including India to Australia — are in preparation. The app shows clearly which routes are live and which are coming soon.

─────────────────────────────────────

LandHere is an information and planning tool, not a legal or immigration service. Immigration rules change — always confirm steps with your university's international student office and with official government sources before acting.
