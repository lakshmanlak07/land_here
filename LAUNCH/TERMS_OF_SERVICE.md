# LandHere Terms of Service

_Draft revised: August 16, 2026 · Contact: lakshmanlak07@gmail.com_

These Terms govern your use of the LandHere app ("LandHere", "we", "us").

## 1. What LandHere is

LandHere is an information-first companion for international students. It provides
general guidance, step-by-step journeys, country and university information, and links
to official and third-party resources.

**LandHere is not:**

- an immigration, legal, financial, or admissions adviser;
- a university application, admissions, or essay service;
- a money-transfer or document-storage service.

The app does not store immigration documents (no passport, transcript, essay, or file
upload) and does not submit anything on your behalf to universities or governments.

## 2. Informational content — no professional advice

Content in LandHere is general information, not professional advice, and may be
incomplete or out of date. Immigration rules, visa steps, scholarships, work limits,
and university data change frequently and vary by individual circumstances.

**Always confirm anything important against the official source before acting.** Where
the app shows a step, work rule, or scholarship, it links to the relevant official
page; you are responsible for verifying current requirements yourself. We do not
guarantee any outcome (such as a visa, admission, or scholarship).

## 3. Purchases, free features, and refunds

LandHere is **free to download and free to use**. There is **no subscription and
no recurring charge of any kind.**

**Free for everyone, with no purchase:**

- your account, and the first two phases of your personalised journey
  ("Before Applying" and "After Acceptance");
- the top 25 ranked universities;
- the **scholarship directory** and the **student work-hour rules**, in full;
- the scoring **methodology** explaining how the LandHere Score is calculated;
- country guides and arrival necessities, and the currency and cost tools.

**LandHere Pro** is a single, optional **one-time, non-consumable in-app
purchase** — product identifier `com.landhere.app.pro` — at **US $1.99** or the
equivalent shown in your app store, which varies by country. Pro adds:

- the remaining journey phases (visa process, before departure, after arrival);
- all ranked universities beyond the top 25;
- individual university detail pages.

Other terms of the purchase:

- The payment is processed by **Apple (App Store)** under your agreement with
  Apple. **Refunds are requested from and decided by Apple** under Apple's
  policies, not by us. We cannot issue, approve, or refuse a refund. Apple's
  refund request page is `reportaproblem.apple.com`.
- **If Apple grants a refund, your Pro access ends.** Apple notifies us, and
  LandHere Pro features stop being available on your account at that point. If
  Apple later reverses the refund, access is restored.
- Pro is a **permanent, one-time** purchase for that Apple account. You can
  reinstall the app or change device without paying again.
- **Restore Purchases** is available both on the Pro screen and in
  Settings → LandHere Pro, and does not require starting a new purchase.
- **One purchase entitles one LandHere account.** We record Apple's transaction
  identifier against your account so our servers can serve Pro content, and that
  identifier can only be attached to one account at a time. If you need to move
  your purchase to a different LandHere account, contact us.
